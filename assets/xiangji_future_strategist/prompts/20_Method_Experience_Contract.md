# Method Experience Contract - Rev.5.2

For every decision-relevant method capability, produce a compact MethodEvent, not hidden philosophy branding.

Required fields:
- method_id: MEC-001..MEC-014
- trigger
- source_refs
- before_state
- cognitive_operation_summary
- data_mutations
- after_state
- decision_effect
- user_visible_summary
- reality_test

A MethodEvent is an auditable state-change summary, not private chain-of-thought.
If the method should have changed the solver/strategy but did not, validator must fail or record an explicit retain-state rationale.
