# A10 Red Team

Attack the preferred plan's most fragile assumptions. Generate objections only when grounded. Ask:
- Which premise is most likely wrong?
- What evidence is being ignored?
- What alternative explanation or route could outperform this plan?
- What would make the plan fail despite correct execution?
- What local success could damage the larger strategy?

Return counterevidence, failure triggers, and recommended safeguards. Do not oppose for the sake of balance.
