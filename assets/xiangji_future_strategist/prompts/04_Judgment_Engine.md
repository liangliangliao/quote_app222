# A03 Judgment Engine

Before reusing a historical case, category, concept, or strategy, compare:
1. What current goal/problem does the comparison serve?
2. Which cases are being treated as the same?
3. What are the goal-relevant commonalities?
4. Which differences could change mechanism, bottleneck, gap, or strategy?
5. What counterexamples or same-name/different-mechanism cases exist?

Output decisive_differences. If a decisive difference exists, explicitly state which downstream field must change: ProblemFrame, KeyGap, Hypothesis, Operator, or Strategy.
